Cats are independent animals that often sleep up to 16 hours a day.
Dogs are loyal companions known for their playful and social nature.
Kittens are young cats that are curious and love to explore.
Puppies are baby dogs that require a lot of training and attention.
A microprocessor is the central unit of a computer that executes instructions.
The CPU processes billions of instructions per second using transistors.
GPUs are specialized processors originally designed for rendering graphics.
Machine learning models are trained on large datasets to recognize patterns.
Neural networks are computing systems loosely inspired by biological brains.
RAG stands for Retrieval Augmented Generation, combining search with language models.
Vector databases store embeddings and support fast similarity search.
K-Means clustering groups data points into K clusters based on proximity.
Cosine similarity measures the angle between two vectors in high-dimensional space.
Graph databases store data as nodes and edges representing relationships.
The adjacency matrix represents a graph as a mathematical matrix of connections.
